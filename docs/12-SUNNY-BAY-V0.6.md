# FisherTown Sunny Bay v0.6 — Moving Fish and Rod

Playable standalone code is served from `public/sunny-bay/` to keep root Fishing Lab v0.4 intact. The previous v0.5 scenic art baked fish, rod and bobber into background. v0.6 replaces underwater scenery with fish-free marine water/caustics/coral and removes the rod and bobber photograph. Five transparent study fish images are extracted from the generated Sunny Bay concept and rendered as fourteen independent actors. Motion includes directional turns, tail strip deformation, approach to bait, bite, hook, fight and recovery. Bites are proximity-triggered instead of pure timers. A separate Canvas draws an articulated graphite rod, cork grip, rotating reel, line, bobber and ripples, responding to hold-to-cast, release and line tension.

Fish pictures are concept illustrations and not scientifically reviewed references. Gameplay data uses provisional marine species names. Preserve commercial-usage/art verification gates. Unit tests: `node --test public/sunny-bay/tests/scene.test.mjs` (three independent logic tests); browser tests: `tests/e2e/sunny-bay.spec.ts`. XAMPP3 standalone installer in the separate download ZIP. Do not replace the approved main site until GitHub Actions CI and Pages deploy both pass.

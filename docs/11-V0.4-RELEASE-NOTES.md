# FisherTown v0.4 — release gates and known limitations

Date: 2026-10-08
Source baseline: v0.3.1 on main (`509da1b0`)

## Functional changes

1. Native portrait **camera crop/pan** replaces the v0.3.1 16:9 miniature scene above controls. Phaser RESIZE fills the actual canvas DOM dimensions, camera preserves 1280×720 world coordinates and follows the bobber or fighting fish.
2. Scene-native landscape HUD is hidden in portrait (including input hit targets). Native DOM controls remain functional and are independent of canvas pixel coordinates. Rotation restores the original in-canvas controls.
3. Catch results are represented by a readable DOM card on portrait and by the original scene card in landscape.
4. Graphics detail selection Auto / Low / Balanced / High controls water-redraw cadence and fish AI fixed step (24/40/60 updates/sec). Auto adjusts based on sustained moving average FPS.
5. Background optimization: source PNG preserved under `public/`, build-time Sharp conversion to 1280×720 WebP at quality 83, redundant PNG removed from dist. Other art originals are not altered.
6. All ten species SVG illustrations received subtle patterns, fin strokes and depth highlights. This is **not a photorealistic release**.

## Automated validation

- `npm run validate` verifies scene structure, each species asset, portrait DOM and PWA orientation.
- `npm run build` runs Sharp WebP optimization, TypeScript compile, Vite build, and dist pruning.
- `npm run smoke` launches production preview in Playwright Chromium: portrait scene geometry, rendered/game-ready state, hook/cast pointer input, quality picker, media asset request and rotation in both directions.
- Both CI and GitHub Pages must pass from the merged main commit.

## Manual device checks still needed

- Android Chrome and Firefox: hold cast, release, tap HOOK, hold/release reel, result and release.
- iOS Safari: change orientation during cast and fight, fullscreen fallback, home-bar safe area and text readability.
- Older mid-range phones: compare low/balanced/high FPS and touch latency over 10-minute sessions.
- Validate quality settings persisted and Fishdex records retained after refresh with storage enabled/blocked.
- Artist review: anatomical correctness and commercial licensing per species before replacing SVG studies with individually reviewed raster art.

## Follow-up art pipeline

Generate/commission **one scientifically matched art source per species**, referencing the taxonomy and distinguishing fins, heads, scales, mouth and pattern. Supply transparent WebP/AVIF gameplay sprite, large catch-card image, and license/source metadata. Do **not** crop generic photo collections and relabel fish, or treat the watermarked or copyrighted online reference images as reusable assets. Avoid loading full-resolution landing art for fish unseen in the scene.

## Known limitations

- Fish art is improved naturalist SVG, still not photo-grade realism. The scene background is produced artwork, not geographic imagery.
- The camera pans automatically; manual swipe-to-pan and re-aim marker are not yet implemented.
- Browser smoke currently confirms entering cast and rotation, not deterministic completion of every possible catch/fight branch. Full-loop real-device playtesting remains an explicit gate.
- No account sync, multiplayer, aquarium or town-building in the Fishing Lab.

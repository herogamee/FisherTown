FisherTown Sunny Bay v0.6 — Asset provenance

The scenic background (assets/sunny-bay-clean.webp) is assembled from the
approved FisherTown concept scenery and algorithmically generated underwater
color, caustic rays and reefs. The static fish, float and rod were removed.

The five transparent marine study sprites in assets/live-fish/*.webp are
individually cropped from the user's approved generated concept art. These are
illustrative game assets, not licensed field photographs or verified biological
reference plates. Each is represented by its own species-oriented gameplay ID.

The rod and reel are generated dynamically inside scene.js and are never
present in the background plate. The lure, line, fighting rod curvature and
reel rotation depend on player actions and game state.

Before commercial species-accurate release, verify body shape, scientific names,
local ecology, reference licenses and colors for each marine species.
